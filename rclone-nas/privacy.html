rclone-nas – Privacy policy

rclone-nas is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google Drive with the drive.file scope only: it can see and change only the files it has created itself, in its owner's Drive.
  • It does not read any other file, e-mail or account data.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings ("Third-party apps with account access").
